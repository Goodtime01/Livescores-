Privacy Policy for Etini LiveScores

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to Etini LiveScores. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Etini LiveScores is designed as a free live sports information portal. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identity details to browse live scores, fixtures, lineups, or statistics.

  • User Feedback & Reviews: If you voluntarily submit a review or feedback through the app, we collect the name, rating, and message you provide. This information is stored securely via Supabase.
  • Match Predictions: Anonymous votes cast on community prediction polls are stored without linking to personal identity.

2. Device Information & Caching

To provide offline functionality and fast loading, our application utilizes local browser storage (Service Workers and LocalStorage) to save favorite teams, recent league views, and cached scores directly on your device.

3. Third-Party Links & Services

Our app provides sports feeds and may contain promotional banners or affiliate links to third-party platforms (such as 1xBet). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external sites.
