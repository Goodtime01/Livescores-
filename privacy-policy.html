Last updated: October 29, 2026

If you allow notifications, we use them to send you goal alerts and match updates. You can turn them off anytime in your phone settings.

The app needs internet to load live scores. We do not track your browsing outside the app.

Our app is for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
